Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the Big Idea Club upload tool (the "App"), a private tool used only by the owner of The Big Idea Club YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the App accesses

  • Basic information about the signed-in YouTube channel (name, ID, subscriber count).
  • The channel's own videos: uploading, scheduling and editing titles, descriptions and tags.
  • YouTube Analytics for the channel's own videos (views, watch time, retention).
  • Comments on the channel's own videos, so the owner can read and reply to them.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to manage The Big Idea Club channel. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared. The App does not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not collect data about viewers or commenters beyond what YouTube shows on the channel's own videos.

Retention and deletion

Analytics and comment data are read on demand and not kept beyond local working notes. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings, which also invalidates the stored token.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at github.com/Calux0098/calux0098.github.io/issues.
